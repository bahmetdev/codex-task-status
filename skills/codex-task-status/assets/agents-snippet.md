## Task titles

Use the installed `codex-task-status` skill when substantive work begins or resumes and before finalizing it. Use the recommended `<Subject> (<state>)` structure unless a custom policy is recorded below. Keep the subject stable; update only a stale suffix on a meaningful evidence-backed transition. Use available task-title controls and verify the result, or propose the title if controls are unavailable. Do not rename on every message or tool call. Routine recurring runs retain their verified schedule suffix. Preserve archived and other tasks unless explicitly asked to change them.

Custom policy, if selected: record the subject language/shape, title format, status labels mapped to their meanings, transition rules, Git/non-Git behavior, and schedule notation here or link a durable policy file. Syntax validation JSON alone is not the semantic policy.
